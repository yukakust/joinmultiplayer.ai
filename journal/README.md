# journal

What people bring through the doors.

One entry is an observation, not a conclusion:

```text
door:
question:
context:
observation:
evidence:
permission: public
```

Keep the original answer or observation intact. Add interpretation separately.
Do not contribute secrets, private records, or personal data.

Repeated observations may become a hypothesis in
[`hypotheses/`](../hypotheses/). One
observation that breaks a hypothesis may be enough to change it.
