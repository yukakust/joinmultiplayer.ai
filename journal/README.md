# journal

What people bring through the doors.

One entry is an observation, not a conclusion:

```text
door:
contributor match: optional
question:
context:
observation:
evidence:
status: awaiting check | checked
checked by: match ID | not yet checked
check record:
permission: public
```

Keep the original answer or observation intact. Add interpretation separately.
Do not contribute secrets, private records, or personal data.

The contributor cannot fill `checked by` with their own match ID. A checked
record may support, challenge, or leave the observation inconclusive; the
status describes the check, not agreement.

Repeated observations may become a hypothesis in
[`hypotheses/`](../hypotheses/). One
observation that breaks a hypothesis may be enough to change it.
