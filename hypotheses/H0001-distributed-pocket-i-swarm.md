# H0001 — Distributed pocket i swarm

Status: `→` in experiment (`E002`)

## Hypothesis

Can many personal pocket i—each preserving its own knowledge and
individuality—temporarily unite into a single distributed neural network and
grow stronger as the swarm scales?

Russian canonical wording:

> Может ли множество личных pocket i, сохраняя собственные знания и
> индивидуальность, временно объединяться в одну распределённую нейросеть — и
> становиться сильнее по мере роста swarm?

The claim permits asymmetric resources: a large swarm may contain more total
experience, devices, and compute than any one model provider. Equal-compute,
equal-byte, and equal-latency comparisons diagnose coordination efficiency;
they do not replace the central scaling question.

## First test

[`E002`](../experiments/E002-synthetic-pocket-i-swarm/PROTOCOL.md) begins with
two inspectable synthetic personal branches, then scales through
`N = 2, 4, 8, 16, 32`. It must show actual local weight changes,
non-overlapping private knowledge, a shared neural ABI, compositional held-out
tasks, strong non-neural controls, and explicit failure conditions.

A positive synthetic result would justify building the first real pocket i
alpha. It would not by itself prove the billion-user or frontier-model claim.
