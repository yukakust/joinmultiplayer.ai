# Claim Hunt 001

**Door:** [`D05`](../doors/#d05)

**Status:** designed, not open. Launch only after `joinmultiplayer.ai/d05`
exists.

## Mission

Ask 30 questions of four AI models. Find a claim the models repeat, trace it
through the internet, and reach its first source—or document where the trail
goes cold.

## Roles

### Scout

Bring a specific claim repeated by an AI model. Preserve the complete answer,
model name, and date.

### Tracer

Take one branch of web sources and follow every citation backward. Preserve
URLs, publication dates, and archived copies when possible.

### Verifier

Read the strongest available primary evidence and record what it actually
supports.

## Candidate record

```text
claim:
models repeating it:
pages repeating it:
earliest source found:
primary evidence:
source trail:
status: orphaned | sourced | disproved | unresolved
```

## Rules

- Never call a claim false before the evidence supports that verdict.
- Keep original answers and sources intact; add interpretation separately.
- Independent-looking pages are not independent evidence until their sources
  are traced.
- Record failed trails. A dead end is part of the result.
- Do not use private, personal, or restricted information.

## Stop conditions

A candidate stops when its primary source is verified, the claim is disproved
by stronger evidence, or every accessible trail has been documented as a dead
end.

The hunt ends after at least one candidate has a reproducible source trail and
a result another person can independently check.

## Before the reveal

If a candidate claim is already familiar to many people, run Source Memory 001
through [`D09`](../doors/#d09) before publishing the source trail. Ask
participants where they remember learning the claim. Revealing the trail first
would contaminate that memory measurement.
